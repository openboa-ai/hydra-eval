# Hydra Eval collaboration contract

- This repository is the independent public research and evidence surface for
  Hydra evaluation. Do not change Hydra from this repository unless a separate,
  explicitly approved work item names that effect.
- Keep documented facts, vendor claims, inferences, open questions, and approved
  decisions visibly separate. Cite primary sources next to material claims.
- Treat a benchmark result as a statement about the full tested configuration,
  not about a model or skill in isolation.
- Quality, tokens, and time are separate observations. Do not invent a combined
  score or threshold before its purpose and trade-offs are approved.
- Do not publish secrets, private repository content, personal data, credentials,
  hidden grader material, or unreviewed raw trajectories.
- Preserve result provenance. Never rewrite an old run as evidence for changed
  task, grader, model, agent, plugin, environment, or source bytes.
- Research may identify candidates and open questions. It does not silently make
  a design, tool-selection, release, or governance decision.
